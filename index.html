About Me

I am Runming Zou (邹润明), a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), Changsha, China, supervised by Prof. Yongqiang Cheng and Associate Prof. Hao Wu. My research interests include statistical signal processing, information geometry and radar signal processing, especially matrix information geometry detection, space-time adaptive processing, and maritime target detection in heterogeneous environments. In addition, distributed radar target detection and anti-jamming are also directions I am interested in.

I am open to discussions and collaborations. Feel free to reach me via Email: nudtzrm@163.com.
